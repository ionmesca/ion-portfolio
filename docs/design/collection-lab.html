The pattern

Stack, Agents & Skills and Articles are the same page three times: the Letter's skeleton — left rail, 640 column — filled with collection rows. The rail is a section wheel that tracks the scroll and can drive it. Every row is an anchor for the preview popover, so the list stays one line per item and the curiosity is paid for on hover, not with more page.

Nothing here is new machinery. The row's hover is the ratified snap rule (0 ms in, 150 ms out). The preview is the popover family verbatim — 150 ms hover intent, 140 ms leave grace, scale .98→1 + fade + 4 px rise on glide 200, one container that morphs between adjacent rows. The wheel's opacity lens is the wheel prototype's own falloff. Only three things are defined here: the row, the credit line, and the install chip.
