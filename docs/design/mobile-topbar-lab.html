A letter

August 2026

I am Ion, a designer and developer based in Barcelona. I work at Ledgy, where I build the tools people use to understand who owns what in a company.

I live with my family and a dog who attends every design review. Most weeks are some mix of Figma, a code editor, and a long walk to sort out whatever is stuck.

History

I got into this the way a lot of people did: a slow family computer and too much time on it. I wanted to change how something looked, found out that you could, and never really stopped.

That turned into design school, then into product work, and eventually into Ledgy. The first thing I built was ugly and it worked, which is still roughly my order of priorities.

Somewhere along the way the two halves stopped being separate jobs. A design that cannot be built is a drawing; code without a point of view is plumbing.

These days I spend most of my time on the equity tools at Ledgy, and on small AI things on the side, mostly to find out what they are actually good for.
