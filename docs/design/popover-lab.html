Popovers

One family. It grows out of the thing you pointed at.

The mechanic

Every surface here is anchored: it starts at the rect of the thing you pointed at and grows to the rect it needs. Between two anchors in the same group it morphs — one container, tweened — instead of closing and reopening. Content crossfades separately, faster, with 2 px of blur. There is no scrim anywhere: the page stays visible, because the page is the context that makes the popover mean anything.

Decoded from Colin Lienard's Contacts.svelte: one persistent container per anchor-group, left/width/height tweened on hover, content flown in with a slight blur, icon tint muted → foreground. Reproduced here with a measured rAF lerp on translate3d + width/height + radius, glide easing, 200 ms in / 150 ms out.

What each demo is asking

1

⌘K as a chip-anchored surface — the decision

Ion's open question was "what happens to the chip?". Three answers, one control.

1 · Morph — the chip becomes the panel, with zero jump. Nothing is duplicated, nothing is remounted, and — the hard rule — nothing in the chip's row ever moves or resizes. The avatar stays 24 px at 5,9. The name stays at 38,11 in the same style. The kbd chip stays where ⌘K sat and crossfades into esc in place. The panel's top-left corner is the chip's top-left corner, and the container grows right and down around that frozen row to 380×444 over 200 ms, radius 15 → 21, Subtle → Overlay. The availability line (green dot, "Available from October") fades in under the row, in space the panel just opened, so it displaces nothing. Close reverses at 150 ms and the panel collapses back into being the chip — again without the row moving.

The price, shown honestly: the esc chip ends up at the chip's right edge, roughly 160 px in, not at the panel's far-right corner where a normal header would put it. Zero jump and a conventionally balanced header cannot both be true. It is left exactly so — judge it by eye.

2 · Regular popover — the chip stays. The conventional answer: the chip remains, lifted (Subtle → Raised, ⌘K brightened), and the panel drops in 8 px below it — scale .98 → 1, fade, rise 4 px, origin top-left, glide 200 ms. Identical content, so the comparison is about motion and continuity only. Note the cost: the identity is now on screen twice.

3 · Modal — the pass-8 mock. 560 centred, 20% scrim, instant both ways.

No scrim on 1 and 2 — tick Page dim 4% to compare that against nothing at all. Trigger = hover is included honestly — 150 ms intent, 300 ms leave grace — so the misfires are feelable: reaching the panel crosses the intro paragraph and the Ledgy link, each of which wants the pointer for itself. In click mode, chip hover is a lift and nothing more.

Social previews — the Colin morph

Three icons, one container. First hover grows it out of that icon (scale .98 → 1, fade, rise 4 px, glide 200 ms). Slide to the next icon and the container morphs — position and size tween, 200 ms — while the card content crossfades through 2 px of blur. The cards are deliberately three different sizes, so the morph is visible rather than theoretical. Leaving collapses at the last anchor in 150 ms. Icons tint muted → foreground; the cards are pointer-enterable through a 140 ms grace corridor.

The family — rules, and where they land

  • Anchored, always. A popover starts at the rect of its trigger and grows. Nothing appears centred in space.rule
  • One container per anchor-group. Moving between siblings morphs it; it never closes and reopens.rule
  • Two ways to grow. Morph: the trigger becomes the surface, corner to corner, and its elements travel into the surface's header. Drop: the trigger stays and a panel appears below it. Morph when the trigger's content belongs in the panel; drop when it does not.morph / drop
  • A morph moves the container, never its anatomy. Shared elements keep their exact position and size through the whole transition; only the surface around them grows. Anything that cannot hold still crossfades in place instead.zero jump
  • Glide 200 ms in, 150 ms out. Container only. Nothing here reaches 400 ms.200 / 150
  • Content is 150 ms and separate — fade + 4–6 px rise + 2 px blur, groups 25 ms apart.150 · blur 2
  • Overlay shadow + 1 px ring whenever a surface is floating. Subtle at rest.shadow-overlay
  • Radius by size: 15 for cards and previews, 21 for panels. 27 stays the landing shell.15 / 21
  • No scrim anywhere. At most a 4% page dim, and only if Ion rules for it.none
  • Hover opens previews. Click opens tools. A hover-opened surface never holds an action you must not misfire.rule
  • Hover intent 150 ms, leave grace 140–300 ms, and cards you can walk the pointer into.150 / grace
